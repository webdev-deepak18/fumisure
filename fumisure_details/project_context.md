## Product in one line
A ready-to-use tin that releases formaldehyde gas (paraformaldehyde 96%, heated by a wax candle). It's used for terminal disinfection of **empty** poultry houses, hatcheries, egg rooms, vehicles, warehouses, labs and stables, after cleaning and disinfection. It replaces the messy, risky formalin + potassium permanganate method.
