### Video issues

**Text / grammar**
- "INDUSTRY'S" → industries; "BACTERIA'S" → bacteria; "bio security" → biosecurity.
- "Popular among **the few** industries" reads as a negative — rephrase (e.g. "Trusted by leading poultry farms").
- "Remove the wick from down" → "Pull out the wick from the bottom".
- "Leave the area comfortably" → "Leave the shed immediately".
- Ending "Kindly use 'FUMISURE'" is weak and drops the ®; no contact/website CTA.
- "Formol" (intro) vs "formaldehyde" (product slide) — pick one.

**Process / safety logic**
- Ventilation is closed twice (first step and last step). Last step should be "close/lock the door".
- Worker lights tins starting near the far door and ends away from the exit — should end nearest the exit.
- Precautions are shown before Directions; PPE reminder is not repeated during lighting.
- Missing: dosage per volume, fumigation duration, airing out, re-entry time (all present in the brochure — see §3).

**Visual consistency**
- Product looks different: white/silver tin with large label (0:30, matches brochure) vs gold/brass tin with different label (1:40 onward).
- Mixed styles: realistic/AI intro, flat slides, cartoon shed exterior, photo shed interior, flat cartoon worker on a photo. Exterior and interior don't look like the same building.
- Worker scale jumps in the last lighting position (huge, covers tins).
- AI watermark (✦ sparkle, bottom-right) visible 0:05–0:30 — remove.
- Caption weight inconsistent ("Close the ventilation…" regular, others bold); justified bullets on Decontamination slide have uneven spacing.
- Leftover artifacts: ghosted "Direction of Use" title over shed scene, ghost tin behind title, diagonal light streak near tin, floating disembodied hand.
- Unbalanced layouts: Safe Handling slide left-heavy; Decontamination slide empty right third until image appears.

**Pacing**
- Lighting scene ~35 s with one static caption — too long.
- Long typewriter reveals leave slides near-empty for several seconds.

**Works well**
- Consistent brand blue/yellow, logo bookends.
- Zig-zag placement diagram is clear.
- Close-up tin steps are easy to follow.

## 2. Brochure – what it says

**Page 1**
- Tagline: "Innovative Fumigation Solutions"; "A good cleaning process will remove only 80% of disease agents"; "Cleaning and Disinfection followed by Effective Fumigation = De-contamination = 100% Safety".
- Product: single-use formaldehyde gas generator; paraformaldehyde 96%; heat depolymerises it, reacts with air moisture → formaldehyde gas.
- Two parts: lower box = wax candle (exothermic), upper box = paraformaldehyde.
- Mode of action: protein denaturation, reacts with amine groups → cell lysis.
- Features: single use, easy to operate, less manpower.
- Cleaning / Disinfection explanation; disinfection depends on cleaning quality, water hardness, disinfectant quality, dilution.

**Page 2**
- Directions: calculate area (ft³ or m³) → choose pack → place zig-zag → room must be airtight → remove label, light candle → PPE → after completion open doors/windows; place birds after **6–8 hrs**.
- Fumigation duration **12–24 hrs**; only empty houses, egg rooms, hatcheries, vehicles, labs, godowns, warehouses, processing plants. Egg storage rooms with eggs: **max 45 min**.
- Area of application: **1X up to 50 m³ · 2.5X up to 125 m³ · 5X 250–500 m³**.
- Shelf life 36 months; packing **10 g, 25 g, 50 g** tins.
- Storage & safe handling + precautions (same as video, plus "avoid inhalation of powder", "wash hands after use").
- 4-step pictogram: place zig-zag → open label → light candle (3–5 min) → leave & close ventilation.
- Contact: Arevin Fine Chemicals India Pvt. Ltd., #998/3, 3rd Floor, Opp. PNB, 3rd Cross, 10th Main, 1st Block, HRBR Layout, Kalyan Nagar, Bengaluru-560043 · +91-80-2542 3006 · info@arevin.com · www.arevin.com

### Brochure issues
- Typo: "Easy to **opearate**".
- "contains paraformaldehyde in **labelled form**" — unclear wording.
- "residual **formal** vapors" → formaldehyde/formalin vapours.
- "which protect disease agent" → "which protect disease agents".
- Claims conflict: "80% cleaning + fumigation = remaining 20%" and "100% decontamination / 100% safety" — absolute claims are risky for a formaldehyde product (regulatory/legal).
- "Assured safety to the birds" — but the product must only be used in **empty** houses; wording can mislead.
- Pack table gap: 1X ≤50, 2.5X ≤125, then 5X starts at **250** — nothing covers 125–250 m³, and "250–500" for 5X doesn't match the linear 1X=50 m³ ratio (5X ≈ 250).
- Duration ambiguity: 12–24 hrs fumigation vs "place birds after 6–8 hrs" — unclear whether 6–8 hrs is after airing out.
- Step 4 has the same "close ventilation" problem as the video (ventilation should already be closed; should say close/lock the door). The video likely copied this.
- Pictogram skips "pull out wick" (the video shows it) and has no step for airing out / re-entry.

## 4. Suggested fixes (priority)

1. Add to video: airtight sealing, empty-premises rule, dosage table, 12–24 hr duration, airing out, re-entry time, contact/CTA end card.
2. Fix last step in both: "Leave the area and lock the door" (ventilation already closed).
3. Use one consistent tin design (the white/silver one from the brochure).
4. Fix grammar/typos listed above in both.
5. Confirm correct pack range with client: 4X/40 g (website) vs 5X/50 g (brochure), and the 125–250 m³ gap.
6. Tone down "100%" claims or add qualifiers.
7. Remove AI watermark and animation artifacts; unify illustration style.
